I am a robotics master's student in Genoa. I like to make things move and observe how they behave. Lately I have been curious about robots that can look at a scene, read a command and “think” about how to act. I am still learning, and this site is where I document my projects, show my passions and keep the notes I take along the way. What I'm curious about

VLA

Vision-Language-Action

The one I've actually worked with. I fine-tuned SmolVLA on a pick-and-place task in simulation and then tried to break it in 11 different ways. I still have a lot to learn, but this is where my interest started.

World Models

World Models

Models that let a robot imagine what happens next before it moves. I find the idea fascinating, but I'm still at the reading stage and haven't built anything with them yet.

RL

Reinforcement Learning

Learning by trial and error. Using this method is how I learned to walk. How hard could it be for a robot?
