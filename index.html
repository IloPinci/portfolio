What I'm building toward

VLA

Vision-Language-Action

How language grounds perception into action - and where that grounding turns out to be brittle under real-world perturbation.

World Models

Learned Simulators

Models that let an agent imagine the consequences of an action before taking it, instead of learning purely from trial and error.

RL

Reinforcement Learning

Closing the loop between prediction and control - turning a model's understanding of the world into decisions that hold up on hardware.
